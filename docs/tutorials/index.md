# Tutorials

Worked projects from start to finish. Each one has its finished pattern, so you
can open it and compare.

- [A straight skirt block](skirt-block.md): the basics of drafting by formula,
  from measurements to two pieces ready to print.
- [A bodice block](bodice-block.md): a close-fitting block with darts,
  armholes, seams checked for length, and notes.
- [A tote bag](tote-bag.md): a lined bag sized by its variables, with
  boxed corners, a pocket and straps; a moved copy for the lining, rounded
  corners, placement lines and materials.
- [A leather card wallet](card-wallet.md): one folded piece with no seam
  allowance, a thumb notch, and stitch holes made with a repeat.
- [Adding a zip](zip.md): a zip in the skirt block's centre back from the
  Features tab, and what one click of Apply makes.
- [Adding a dart](darts.md): a second dart in the skirt block's back from
  the Features tab, and a cap on every dart's seam allowance.
- [Adding a hem](hem.md): the skirt block's front and back hems in one step
  from the Features tab, sharing their depth, with ends that lie flat.
- [Adding a facing](facing.md): the bodice block's front and back neck
  facings in one step from the Features tab, seamed at the shoulder, with
  an interfacing.
- [Adding buttons](buttons.md): the bodice block's front opened and
  buttoned in one step from the Features tab, with buttonholes and an
  extension that follow the number of buttons.

For finished patterns without the steps, see
[Example patterns](../getting-started/examples.md).
